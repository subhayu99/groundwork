# Groundwork Platform Audit — 2026-06-08

Synthesis of the verified findings across 36 review units (programming-basics, algorithms, data-structures, the scene engine, PanZoom, the tier map, navigation/registry, the notation/glossary bridge, and the landing/Chrome shell). Rejected findings have already been removed upstream; everything below traces to a confirmed finding with a file:line and quoted evidence.

## Executive summary

The lesson content is largely strong and the scene engine works, but the audit surfaces a small cluster of genuinely broken or misleading items that should be fixed first. Two engine-level defects have platform-wide blast radius: `beat.arrows` are silently never rendered in `SceneLayout` (so every connector arrow authored across 19 topic files is invisible), and the graphs lesson's canvas graph (8 nodes / 11 edges) does not match `algorithm.py` (6 nodes / 5 edges), breaking the non-negotiable left/right-sync principle outright. Other criticals are concrete content errors: a backwards monotonic-stack temperature description, a self-contradicting folder total in recursion, and a wrong set-operation complexity (`O(min)` applied to union/difference). The single largest recurring theme is glossability: ~35 distinct distinctive terms are used in user-visible prose without a `<Term>` chip or glossary key, plus several adjective/noun mismatches (`amortized` vs `amortization`, `monotonic` vs `monotonicity`) that defeat the exact-match lookup. The second recurring theme is left/right code-sync drift — `codeLabels` and `onActiveLine` highlighting lines that contradict (or omit) what the visual is teaching. Accessibility hygiene on the home `TopicTierMap` and Chrome shell is the third theme: missing focus rings on SVG chips, no skip link, gated/disabled Next button with no announced reason, and sub-legible SVG text at the `md` breakpoint. Three findings are best confirmed with a live check (popover overflow-clipping inside the WhyCard, the WhyCard overflow clip frequency, and a couple of SVG-`<title>` AT-support claims) and are flagged inline.
